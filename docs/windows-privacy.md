# Vyrra Desktop for Windows privacy

Runner Concepts LLC makes Vyrra Desktop. You can dictate without an account.

## Dictation and saved text

Vyrra processes microphone audio and WAV files you choose on your PC. Speech recognition runs locally. When you copy a result, Windows puts it on your clipboard. When you use a shortcut to insert a result, the receiving app gets the text.

Choosing **Save to Library** stores a transcript in Vyrra’s local app data. Your vocabulary, snippets and preferences also stay on the device. To remove a saved transcript, open its actions in **Library** and choose **Delete transcript**. Archiving keeps it available in the archive. You manage original WAV files and copies you put into other apps separately.

## Model downloads

If you choose **Download speech model** or install the optional writing model online, Vyrra downloads model files from Hugging Face. The download service receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. You can also select model files you already have. Vyrra includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

## Meeting notes

You choose the calendar invitations to import and the calls to schedule. For local notes on Windows, Vyrra opens the selected meeting link and captures your microphone and Windows system output. This can include sound from other apps. Tell the people in the call before capture starts. The floating stop control ends capture. The local transcript and any summary made with the local writing model stay on your computer.

If you choose a self-hosted participant, Vyrra sends the meeting link, scheduled time, participant name and picture to the server you configure. The participant requests captions from the meeting service. Vyrra can retrieve the transcript and notes from that server. Windows Credential Manager stores the server API key. Deleting local notes removes the copy on your PC. The server operator controls the server copy, and the meeting provider applies its own privacy practices.
